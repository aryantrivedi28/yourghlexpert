## 📱 Mobile-First Approach

**Breakpoints:**
- Mobile: 375px+ (default)
- Tablet: 640px (sm breakpoint)
- Desktop: 1024px (lg breakpoint)
- Wide: 1280px (xl breakpoint)

**Touch Targets:**
- Minimum 48×48px
- 4px gap between buttons
- Large click areas

**Performance:**
- LCP < 2.5s
- FID < 100ms
- CLS < 0.1
- Mobile Lighthouse 95+
